A scene's first frame waits for the page's fonts (before the build, so canvas text uses them) and for every `<img>` it shows to decode, so a video export opens on the picture. A `threeScene` build may be async, to load textures before the first frame. The presmium-animate skill adds `gallery/catalog`: 80 scenes adapted from the HyperFrames catalog (transitions and camera moves, annotation and pointers, product screens, code and data, captions and type, motion, shaders and 3D) whose text, images and data are parameters, indexed in "Find an effect"; scene CSS is scoped to its frame; the story, length and asset references gain a brief with options and approval gates, sound and music, rhythm and self-review.
