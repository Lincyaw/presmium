The document editor fills its page: a press below the last block puts the caret at the end of the document, in its empty last paragraph or in a new one. A page's empty last paragraph shows the typing hint even without the caret. New Page selects the new page's default title, so the author's first words name it, and Enter at the end of a title goes into the page's empty body paragraph instead of adding another.
