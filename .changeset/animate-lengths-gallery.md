The slide layout `full` gives the whole slide to its content: no chapter, title or footer (the title stays in the navigation, the outline and for assistive technology), so a scene can fill a slide and a lecture video frame. Two colour tokens give animations light and shade that hold in both appearances: `sheen` stays light and `shadow` stays dark. The presmium-animate skill adds animation by length (1, 5, 10, 30 and 60 seconds): what each second shows, how short pieces compose into films, how to make short moves look polished (anticipation, calibrated overshoot, reactions, light) and how to export and cut them, with a gallery (`gallery/lengths`) of 42 scenes, among them 24 polished atoms, four per library, each shown once with its beat sheet, and four 30- and 60-second films built from them (`gallery/films`). `references/libraries.md` gains the rules every scene follows and the pitfalls met while building them.
