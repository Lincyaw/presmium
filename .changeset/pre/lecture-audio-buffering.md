Prebuffer narrated playback as one sequence of audio and animated pictures: warm a bounded 30-second window, wait for playable audio and prepared pictures before starting or crossing a step, and hold the timeline while buffering. Replaying a finished clip resets its media position. Open single-track lecture links directly in the player and record the source CLI version and commit in the repository's Pages build.
