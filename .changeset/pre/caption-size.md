Captions have a size for the whole document: `production.captions.scale` (0.7 to 1.6, default 1) multiplies the presentation's caption overlay and the captions burned into an exported lecture video, and Video Production's Captions tab sets it with **Caption size** / “字幕大小” (70% to 160% in steps of 10%). Burned captions are now about 60 px on a 1080p frame at the default size.
