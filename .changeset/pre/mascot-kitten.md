Presmium has a mascot: a black Bombay kitten in silhouette that acts out what the AI Assistant is doing.

- `@lincyaw/design/mascot` plays it: 22 actions drawn as key poses with their timing (sitting, walking, running, grooming, stretching, sleeping, and the assistant's thinking, reading, writing, asking and done), a black cat on light backgrounds and a white one on dark. `mascotFrame` is a function of the time, so a film can seek it. The sheets are generated by `pnpm --filter @lincyaw/design mascot` from the poses in `tools/mascot`.
- In the workbench it sits in the chat's header (thinking, reading, writing, waiting for approval, done, then sitting, lying down and asleep as time passes), in the notice while the AI changes the document, walks to the places the AI changed in the editor and the preview, and runs along an export's progress bar.
- Settings → General → Kitten shows or hides it everywhere.
- The preview reports where the changed blocks are while they blink (`flashed`).
