`presmium-present` is merged into `presmium-author`, which now covers writing, presenting and producing a document. Its writing guide gives each page a reading version (formal written language, in Chinese or English word order, every sentence carrying information) and a presentation version (a claim as heading, keywords, pictures and steps, with a separate spoken script as narration), shows how `only` keeps their words apart, and ends with a review from a fresh reader's seat. The skill lists the MCP author actions in the order an agent uses them; classroom and lecture production move into it, and the two publishing guides are one.
