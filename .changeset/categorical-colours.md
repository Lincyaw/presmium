Diagrams and animations have categorical colours for the things they tell apart (roles, series, categories): `--pm-color-category-1` to `--pm-color-category-8`, each with a `-soft` fill, with light and dark values, in `COLORS` and in scenes as `color('category-1')`. They carry identity, not status. Taken in order, neighbours stay apart for colour-blind readers and so does any pair among the first four; each reads as a line or as text on the page and on a surface. The k-means scene in the animation gallery colours its clusters with them instead of the status colours.
