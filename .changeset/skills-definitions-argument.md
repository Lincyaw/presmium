The writing guide and the guide for pieces made by several agents now ask for a term's definition at its first mention, recorded in the term table with the page where it first appears and the calques not to use (对照组, not 臂, for an experiment's arm). A closing list of what the work cannot show counts as a defensive sentence; the work states what it answers instead. The review reads the argument as a chain, each page's claim against the pages it rests on. Each agent rereads its own words against the storyboard before reporting, and the cold read adds a third pass on definitions, information and the argument.
